About FruitAI

Intelligent Fruit Classification & Quality Grading

FruitAI is an AI-powered application designed to identify fruit types and assess their visible quality using deep learning and computer vision. It combines image classification with Multi-Task Learning to predict fruit identity and quality from a single image.

Our Objective

To simplify fruit inspection by providing fast, consistent, and interpretable visual predictions that can support fruit sorting and quality assessment.

Supported Fruits

  • Apple
  • Banana
  • Guava
  • Lime
  • Orange
  • Pomegranate

Quality Categories

  • Good: The fruit appears to have a good visible surface condition.
  • Bad: The fruit shows visual characteristics associated with poor quality.
  • Mixed: The image contains mixed or uncertain visible quality characteristics.

AI Technology

FruitAI explores a ResNet50 backbone enhanced with Convolutional Block Attention Module (CBAM) and Multi-Task Learning (MTL). The architecture predicts fruit type and quality through two output heads.

  • Deep Learning: Extracts visual features from fruit images.
  • CBAM Attention: Helps the model emphasize relevant channel and spatial features.
  • Multi-Task Learning: Predicts fruit identity and quality within a shared architecture.
  • Grad-CAM: Helps visualize image regions that influence model predictions.
  • Vision-Language Model: Generates a human-readable explanation based on the image and model predictions.

How It Works

  1. Upload a fruit image or select an example image.
  2. The image is resized and preprocessed.
  3. The trained deep learning model predicts the fruit type and quality category.
  4. The application displays predictions and confidence scores.
  5. Available AI explanations and visualizations help users interpret the result.

Our Vision

To explore accessible AI-assisted fruit inspection and contribute to more consistent, transparent, and efficient quality assessment.

Important: FruitAI assesses visible appearance only. It cannot confirm internal freshness, detect every hidden defect, or determine whether a fruit is safe to eat.
